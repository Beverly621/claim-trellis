# Literature benchmark policy

## Inclusion rules

Seed literature must be:

- written in English;
- published from 2022 through 2026;
- an original research article in a high-authority field journal;
- identified by DOI and a first-party journal URL;
- represented by metadata and editor-authored evidence paraphrases, not redistributed PDFs;
- checked against the article abstract or full text and dated in the benchmark record.

The seed currently spans artificial intelligence for biology, genomics, cardiometabolic
medicine, neurology, hematology, and obesity medicine.

## Copyright boundary

The repository does not bundle publisher PDFs or substantial source text. Evidence fields
are concise original paraphrases with locators and links. Runtime users must supply or
lawfully retrieve the documents they are entitled to process.

## Case design

Each source contributes a supported case and at least one controlled hard negative. Hard
negatives alter a material dimension such as direction, population, endpoint, duration,
or causal strength. Labels are editor-authored and require independent annotation before
they can support accuracy claims.
